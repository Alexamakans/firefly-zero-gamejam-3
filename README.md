Uploaded on itch.io: https://ligosoith.itch.io/ghastly-goats-gun-induced-ghostly-gait

Meant to be played on a Firefly Zero.

The ghastly goat's life was cut short by a dastardly bastard with a gun. He now takes out his frustrations on the witches and bats up high in the sky; watching out for portals to purgatory looking to suck in any unsuspecting ghosts.

Controls:

    South/Z: Jump (From ground, and twice again in the air)
    East/X: Dash (Infinite times on the ground, and twice again in the air)

Hazards:

    Platforms can end the run if you run into their sides.
    Portals end the run if you aren't dashing when colliding with them.

Goodies:

    Bats & Witches give extra points.
    Portals give extra points (need to be dashing).

All assets made by me except the gunshot sound (https://sfxmint.com/sounds/impact-gunshot) and the font (https://fonts.fireflyzero.com/ascii).
